MynaBooks

A voice-driven audiobook player for people losing their sight.

Download on the App Store Watch the demo

About

MynaBooks is an iPhone audiobook player for people who can no longer see the screen and won't be learning a screen reader. This is usually someone losing their sight later in life, with a family caregiver helping them.

To use it, you hold anywhere on the screen and speak, and the app answers out loud. Nothing has to be aimed at, tapped precisely, or seen. A caregiver chooses the books, so the shelf stays short: family recordings, ripped CDs, or free public-domain audiobooks from LibriVox.

What I built

  • Voice control with on-device speech recognition, so no account is needed and nothing is sent anywhere
  • Shelves from shared folders and LibriVox, with offline downloads for car journeys
  • PDF read-aloud: hospital leaflets, manuals and family histories read in chapters
  • A caregiver view showing what was said, what the app heard, and what it played
  • Siri App Intents, spoken help, and a caregiver cheatsheet

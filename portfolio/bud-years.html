Bud Years

Steady daily practice for the tests and competitions that shape your next few years.

Visit budyears.com

About

Bud Years helps students prepare for exams and competitions through steady, continuous practice rather than last-minute cramming. Twenty minutes a day beats a weekend of cramming, because what you revisit is what you keep.

It covers SAT®, PSAT/NMSQT® and ACT® preparation, including Vocabulary, Reading & Writing, Math and Test Strategy, as well as Trivia and Logic sets. AP®, GRE®, GMAT® and Olympiad material is in preparation.

What I built

  • A paced daily set that introduces new material at a rate a student can keep up with
  • Review sets that bring back missed questions until they are answered correctly on two separate days
  • Courses, subjects and lessons all loaded from the backend (Firestore), with nothing tied to a particular exam
  • Offline use with bundled fallback content, and no account or sign-in

MetaSight

Indoor orientation for blind users, spoken through Meta glasses.

About

MetaSight helps a blind person find their bearings indoors. A caregiver puts printed AprilTag markers on the walls and records what is near each one. The app reads whichever markers the glasses' camera can see and speaks the orientation out loud, for example "you are facing the kitchen door; the balcony is behind you".

It is deliberately not a mobility aid. It doesn't detect obstacles or traffic, and it never says a route is safe. The cane or the guide dog does that. It also has no map: the markers are the map, because every removed subsystem is one that could have given a confident wrong answer.

What I built

  • SignpostKit, the tested core logic that turns the markers it sees into spoken orientation
  • A prototype that streams live video from Meta glasses to an iPhone, built to test the camera path
  • A measurement plan for how well AprilTags are detected from a body-worn camera while walking
  • Distribution through TestFlight, because Meta's glasses toolkit can't yet ship on the App Store
